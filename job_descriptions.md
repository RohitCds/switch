## 1. Cisco — Software Engineer (AI)

### Role Overview
- **Position:** Software Engineer (AI)
- **Company:** Cisco
- **Team / Domain:** Partner Incentives and Metrics Component
- **Target Experience:** Bachelor’s + 3+ years or Master’s + 2+ years

### Impact & Responsibilities
- **Component Ownership:** Drive knowledge, end-to-end delivery, and maintenance for the *Partner Incentives and Metrics* component—ensuring accurate data pipelines, automation, and reliable production feature behavior.
- **End-to-End AI Solution Delivery:** Design, develop, and unit test AI-enabled features utilizing Cisco Preferred LLM, Cisco LLM, and SDLC Agents, leveraging Copilot for Code to accelerate implementation.
- **Production Support & Optimization:** Investigate and resolve bugs, performance bottlenecks, data discrepancies, and security concerns, using AI-augmented workflows to minimize SDLC cycle time.
- **Process & Scope Definition:** Establish engineering best practices and scope technical deliverables using AI-driven development processes.
- **Long-Term Maintainability:** Maintain and enhance production AI solutions to guarantee high availability, reliability, and architectural cleanliness.
- **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Partner closely with Business Systems Analysts (BSA) and Product Owners (PO) to execute prioritized roadmap items across Solution and Execution phases in the Ops Model.

### Minimum Qualifications
- **Education & Experience:** Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, AI/ML Engineering, or related field with 3+ years of professional experience, OR Master’s degree with 2+ years of professional experience.
- **Architecture & GenAI:** Proven understanding of Generative AI capabilities, N-Tier architectural concepts, Harness Engineering, and their direct impact on the *Code → Design → Test → Deploy* lifecycle.
- **Core Languages & Tech:** Strong programming proficiency in **Python**, **Java**, and **Full-Stack Development**.
- **Data & Distributed Systems:** Hands-on experience designing and building distributed backend systems using **Snowflake**.
- **ML Fundamentals:** Knowledge of data preprocessing, feature engineering, and model evaluation techniques.
- **Methodology & Tooling:** 
  - Agile software development lifecycle.
  - Development and DevOps infrastructure: Jira, Confluence, Jenkins, CI/CD pipelines.
  - AI & Automation Tooling: GitHub Copilot, BOTs/Agents, and test automation frameworks.
- **Soft Skills:** Strong communication, organizational skills, and demonstrated success working in collaborative team settings.

### Preferred Qualifications
- Advanced application of Harness Engineering and N-Tier architecture to production GenAI systems.
- Advanced distributed data modeling and optimization on Snowflake.
- Track record of delivering AI-driven internal automation and developer productivity agents.

## 2. Salesforce — Forward Deployed Engineer

### Role Overview
- **Position:** Forward Deployed Engineer (AI / Agentic Solutions)
- **Company:** Salesforce
- **Team / Domain:** Agentforce Customer Delivery & Frontier AI Deployments
- **Target Experience:** 10+ years of software engineering / technical delivery experience
- **Travel Requirement:** ~25% travel to customer sites

### About the Role & Builder Mindset
Salesforce is hiring **Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs)**—experienced Technical Builders who design, build, and deploy agentic AI solutions directly inside enterprise customer production environments. You work within a delivery pod, collaborating closely with a **Deployment Strategist** (Strategic Builder) to co-create high-impact AI systems that go live from day one.

Engagements range from dedicated large-scale strategic accounts to multi-account deployments spanning configuration, customization, and full-stack engineering.

### The Builder Experience
- **AI-Powered Development:** Build real-world customer solutions using modern developer tooling (Cursor, Claude, and Salesforce internal tools like Vibes).
- **Collaborative Delivery:** Pair with Senior FDEs and Deployment Strategists to own technical components end-to-end with structured mentorship and leadership pathways.
- **Frontier Access:** Build with next-generation capabilities (Agentforce, Data 360, platform, and headless architectures) and provide real-time field feedback directly to core product teams.
- **Continuous Innovation:** Experiment with emerging AI models, agent frameworks, and orchestration paradigms to educate internal teams and clients.

### Key Responsibilities
- **Agent Intelligence Design:** Build autonomous agents that execute real actions for global enterprises. Design prompts, reasoning chains, dynamic tool calls, and API integrations.
- **End-to-End Component Ownership:** Drive solutions from architectural design through deployment, automated validation, and production observability.
- **Data Engineering & Pipelines:** Build and integrate scalable data pipelines across **Salesforce Data 360**, **Snowflake**, **Databricks**, and client data platforms.
- **Dashboards & Observability:** Develop agent performance dashboards, telemetry trackers, and business KPI reporting to monitor runtime health and return on investment.
- **Rapid Prototyping:** Build MVPs and POCs that transition from initial architecture sketches to production-ready deployments in days.
- **Customer Co-Building:** Co-develop alongside customer engineering teams, running enablement sessions to upskill their internal developers.
- **Product Feedback Loop:** Identify platform edge cases, capability gaps, and architectural needs to inform Salesforce's core agent platform roadmap.

### Role Requirements
- **Experience:** 10+ years of software engineering or technical delivery experience with at least one flagship production system you can walk through end-to-end.
- **Customer-Facing Delivery:** Prior technical consulting, professional services, or forward deployed engineering experience (**mandatory requirement**).
- **Programming Languages:** Fluent in at least one of **Python**, **JavaScript/TypeScript**, **Java**, or **Apex** (with agility to adopt others).
- **LLMs & Prompt Engineering:** Deep hands-on experience with LLMs, prompt debugging, token optimization, and failure mode analysis.
- **Data Architecture & APIs:** Strong foundation in dimensional data modeling, REST/gRPC APIs, enterprise integration patterns, and distributed pipelines.
- **Executive Communication:** Ability to lead technical design discussions with customer architects, CTOs, and sales leaders.
- **Engineering Rigor:** High standards for test coverage, CI/CD deployment hygiene, and systematic evaluation of non-deterministic LLM outputs.
- **Education:** Degree in Computer Science or a related quantitative field.

### Nice-to-Haves
- Salesforce platform experience or certifications (*Administrator*, *Platform Developer I*, *Agentforce Specialist*).
- Familiarity with agent orchestration frameworks (**LangChain**, **LlamaIndex**).
- Hands-on data engineering on **Snowflake**, **Databricks**, or **Google BigQuery**.
- Specialized domain expertise in data engineering, headless platform architectures, or enterprise security.

## 3. Apple — AI / Machine Learning Engineer

### Role Overview
- **Position:** AI / Machine Learning Engineer
- **Company:** Apple
- **Team / Domain:** Artificial Intelligence and Data Platform Team
- **Target Experience:** Bachelor’s / Master’s + 4+ years of professional experience
- **Business Scope:** Retail, iTunes, Marketing, AppleCare, Operations, Finance, Sales

### Role Description
The Artificial Intelligence and Data Platform team provides enterprise-grade data services, analytics, reporting, and AI solutions across Apple's core business units. As an AI/ML Engineer, you will build intelligent systems to democratize AI across the company, designing SOTA models, scalable ML infrastructure, and production inference engines.

### Key Responsibilities
- **Production ML Systems:** Design and deploy state-of-the-art AI systems that enhance Apple's product ecosystem and business operations.
- **Scalable ML Infrastructure:** Build robust data processing, storage, and model serving infrastructure for low-latency, high-throughput production inference.
- **Data & Feature Engineering:** Develop novel feature engineering, data augmentation pipelines, prompt engineering strategies, and fine-tuning frameworks.
- **Automated ML Pipelines:** Implement automated end-to-end MLOps pipelines covering preprocessing, feature store updates, model training, hyperparameter tuning, and offline/online evaluation.
- **Model Optimization & Compression:** Apply model compression techniques (quantization, pruning, distillation) to reduce parameter footprint while maintaining high benchmark performance.
- **End-to-End Solution Lifecycle:**
  1. Understand complex business challenges.
  2. Collaborate across engineering, data science, and business units.
  3. Design statistical or deep learning solutions.
  4. Develop and lead technical execution.
  5. Deploy to production with rigorous data governance and security compliance.

### Minimum Qualifications
- **Experience & Education:** Bachelor’s degree (or equivalent) with **4+ years** of hands-on experience in AI/ML & Data Engineering.
- **Data-to-Action Architecture:** Demonstrated ability to build systems transforming raw data $\rightarrow$ structured insights $\rightarrow$ autonomous actions.
- **RAG Systems:** Deep experience designing and deploying **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** pipelines incorporating structured data, user personas, and domain-specific knowledge into LLM contexts.
- **Vector Search & Embeddings:** Deep understanding of embedding models, vector databases, indexing strategies (HNSW, IVFFlat), and semantic retrieval algorithms.
- **Agentic Workflows:** Practical experience constructing autonomous AI agents with memory systems, planning modules, tool-use execution loops, and orchestration frameworks (**LangChain**, **LlamaIndex**).
- **Transformer Architecture:** In-depth knowledge of transformer mechanics (self-attention, cross-attention, positional encodings) and practical experience with **BERT**, **GPT**, and **LLaMA** models (fine-tuning, LoRA/QLoRA, prompt optimization).
- **Deep Learning Frameworks:** Proficiency with **PyTorch** or **TensorFlow** for model training, optimization, and export (e.g., ONNX, TensorRT, TorchScript).
- **Big Data at Scale:** Experience handling petabyte-scale datasets using distributed computing engines with **Python** and **Scala**.
- **Databases & Data Modeling:** Strong database development skills in relational and MPP/distributed data platforms (**Snowflake**, **SingleStore**) alongside advanced SQL and dimensional data modeling.

### Preferred Qualifications
- Proven track record building **multi-agent collaborative systems** that coordinate autonomous reasoning, retrieval, and action execution across distributed tools.
- Deep hands-on experience with modern cloud data warehouses, data lakes, and public cloud infrastructure (**AWS** preferred, or **GCP** / **Azure**).
- Strong prototyping velocity to design, test, and ship proof-of-concept (POC) ML solutions for emerging enterprise problems.

## 4. HPE (Hewlett Packard Enterprise) — AI / Machine Learning Engineer

### Role Overview
- **Position:** AI / Machine Learning Engineer
- **Company:** Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE)
- **Job Family:** Integrated Software Algorithms & Predictive Systems
- **Management Level:** Intermediate Level (Independent execution under moderate supervision)
- **Target Experience:** 2–4 years of experience

### Role Summary
Develops and programs software algorithms to structure, analyze, and extract insights from structured and unstructured data across HPE product and systems applications. Applies machine learning, statistical modeling, and deep learning technologies to optimize system performance, data management, and operational accuracy.

### Key Responsibilities
- **Model Design & Implementation:** Research, experiment with, and deploy machine learning models and algorithms to solve specific business and systems engineering challenges.
- **Data Preparation & Preprocessing:** Clean, normalize, transform, and extract features from large, diverse real-world datasets to feed training pipelines.
- **Training & Hyperparameter Tuning:** Train ML models, optimize hyperparameters, run cross-validation, and evaluate results using standard metrics (Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, ROC-AUC).
- **Deep Learning & Computer Vision / NLP:** Apply, optimize, and scale deep learning algorithms enabling systems to visualize, interpret, and respond to complex real-world environments.
- **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Work alongside data scientists, software engineers, and business stakeholders to translate functional requirements into high-performance code.
- **Testing, Debugging & Documentation:** Author procedures for deployment, maintenance, and monitoring; establish system performance metrics.
- **Design Reviews & Communication:** Present technical findings and architectures during design reviews and stand-ups; author reports and maintain business intelligence dashboards.

### What You Need to Bring
- **Mathematical & Statistical Foundation:** Solid understanding of linear algebra, multivariate calculus, probability theory, and inferential statistics for model analysis and evaluation.
- **Programming & Libraries:**
  - Languages: **Python**, **R**, or **Java**.
  - ML/DL Frameworks: **TensorFlow**, **PyTorch**, **scikit-learn**, **Keras**.
  - Data Querying: **SQL** for dataset extraction and manipulation.
- **Core ML Algorithms:** Strong command over classical algorithms (linear/logistic regression, decision trees, random forests, SVMs, gradient boosting) as well as modern deep neural network architectures.
- **Data Quality & Feature Engineering:** Practical experience diagnosing real-world data quality anomalies and developing robust feature pipelines.
- **Software Engineering Best Practices:** Familiarity with Git version control, unit testing, agile development, and production ML model deployment.
- **Communication:** Strong ability to explain complex technical and algorithmic concepts clearly to cross-functional peers and non-technical stakeholders.

### Education & Experience Requirements
- **Education:** Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, or a closely related quantitative discipline (Master’s degree is desirable).
- **Experience:** Typically **2–4 years** of relevant industry, internship, or applied research experience.
